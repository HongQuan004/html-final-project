About Michigan

Michigan is a state located in the Great Lakes region of the Upper Midwestern United States. It is famous for its natural beauty, rich industrial history, and extensive freshwater coastline.

To learn more about travel destinations, visit the Official Pure Michigan Travel Site .

Top 5 Michigan Quick Facts

  • Great Lakes Coastline: Michigan has the longest freshwater coastline of any political subdivision in the world.
  • Two Peninsulas: It is the only U.S. state to consist of two distinct peninsulas (Upper and Lower).
  • State Tree: The official state tree is the Eastern White Pine.
  • Automotive Capital: Detroit, Michigan is historically recognized as the heart of the American automotive industry.
  • No Spot Far From Water: You are never more than 6 miles from a natural water source anywhere in the state.
